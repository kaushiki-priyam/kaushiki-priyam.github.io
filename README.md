## About me

I'm a product designer working at the intersection of UX, product design, and research, currently at Roku. I design for the TV screen, where every interaction has to work from ten feet away with a remote.

I don't stop at the mockup. I prototype in code, build my own tools, and ship what I design, including this site, which is hand-coded in plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with no frameworks or templates.

What that means in practice:

- **0-to-1 execution:** taking an idea from a hallway chat to a working, testable product, shaping the concept, the design, and the first build
- **Design:** product thinking, interaction design, and visual craft, grounded in research
- **Build:** working prototypes and front-end code, so ideas get tested as real experiences
- **Bridge:** a shared language with engineers, which makes handoff faster and the shipped product closer to the intent

## Design philosophy

I lean on a first-principles approach: I always ask "Why?" and look for the business and user impact in the products I create.
